Check the originality of your project idea

Your draft is compared with every project in the institutional repository by meaning, not just matching words. Checks are private and unlimited; nothing is shared with faculty until you press Submit on the report.

How the score works
  • Your title and abstract are converted into a sentence embedding (Sentence-BERT).
  • Cosine similarity is computed against every stored project.
  • Originality = 100 − highest similarity.
  • Original below 55% similarity, Related 55–75%, High overlap 75% and above.
  • Every report gets a code that faculty can verify.
